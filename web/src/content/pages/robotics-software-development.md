We develop software for industrial robot programming and fabrication, including CAD and CAM integrations and standalone applications.

## Robot programming {#programming-and-processes}

- Robot kinematics and motion simulation.
- Toolpath generation from design geometry and process parameters.
- Controller-code generation and equipment integration.
- Process control and interfaces for fabrication and research applications.

## Selected work {#selected-work}

[Robots](/projects/robots#references) has been used at more than 70 universities and design schools. Published applications include construction, manufacturing and robotic measurement.

Our [robotic 3D-printing work](/projects#software-for-robotic-furniture-production) covers slicing, toolpath generation, robot simulation and extrusion control.

[Six-axis robotic milling](/projects#cam-integration-for-six-axis-milling) connects CAD and CAM tools with robot simulation and controller-code generation for six-axis machining.

## Robots integration {#robots-and-integrations}

We extend [Robots' programming and simulation tools](/projects/robots) and integrate its .NET library into custom applications.
