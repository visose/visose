We build software for industrial robot programming, simulation and process control in production and research.

## Robot programming {#programming-and-processes}

- Robot kinematics and motion simulation.
- Motion planning and toolpath generation.
- Controller-code generation and equipment integration.
- Process control and operator interfaces.

## Integration and delivery {#robots-and-integrations}

We build standalone applications, extend existing CAD/CAM and production software, and develop libraries for teams building their own tools. We work with multiple industrial robot platforms and connect the software to the controllers and equipment the process requires.

## Selected work {#selected-work}

[Robots](/projects/robots), our open-source programming and simulation software, has been used at more than 70 universities and design schools. Its [published applications](/projects/robots#references) include construction, manufacturing and robotic measurement.

Our [robotic 3D-printing work](/projects#software-for-robotic-furniture-production) includes printing in flat and curved layers, synchronizing extrusion with actual robot speed and coordinating multiple robots.

[Six-axis robotic milling](/projects#cam-integration-for-six-axis-milling) uses custom post-processors to translate cutter position, orientation and feed rate from existing CAM tools into robot motion and controller programs.

## Discuss your project {#discuss-a-workflow}

Tell us about the robot, equipment and process you want to control. We develop the software remotely. Your team operates the equipment and validates programs on the robot cell.
