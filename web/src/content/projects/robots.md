We created and maintain Robots, an open-source system for programming industrial robots from Grasshopper and .NET. Its core checks and simulates toolpaths against a model of the robot cell, then generates controller-specific code. Cell models can include multiple arms, tracks and positioners.

Originally developed at UCL, Robots has been used at more than 70 universities and design schools worldwide. Published research and independent extensions cover construction, manufacturing and robotic measurement.
