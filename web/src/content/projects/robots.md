We created and maintain Robots, an open-source library and Grasshopper plugin for industrial robot programming. The work spans robot kinematics, motion simulation, controller-code generation and integration with Rhino and custom .NET applications.

Originally developed at UCL, Robots has been used at more than 70 universities and design schools worldwide. Published research and independent extensions cover construction, manufacturing and robotic measurement.
